### The through-line

Most of the field argues that AI improves analytics. I think the under-told direction is the
interesting one: **behavioral science directly improves AI.** An AI that judges people is a rater,
and a century of psychometrics already knows how to make a noisy rater reliable. Three provisional
patents (2026) and the portfolio are the argument.
